# Community conduct

Be respectful, clear and constructive. Review the proposed change rather than the contributor. Harassment, discrimination, disclosure of another person's private information and unwanted advertising are not permitted.

Maintainers have discretion to moderate content that violates these expectations. Report concerns through GitHub's private reporting channel. If private reporting is unavailable, follow the fallback in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not post sensitive details in public issues.
